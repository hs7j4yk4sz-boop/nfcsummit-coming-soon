27–29 May 2027Unicorn Factory · Lisbon
Ask a question

The summit

When and where is NFC Summit 2027?

From Thursday 27 to Saturday 29 May 2027, at Unicorn Factory Lisboa, Rua da Manutenção 118, in Beato, Lisbon. More about the venue.

What is NFC Summit?

The digital pop culture festival of Lisbon: digital art, trading cards, talks, a hackathon and parties, in one place, once a year. It began in 2022 as the Non Fungible Conference. 2027 is the sixth edition. More about NFC.

What is the programme?

Thursday 27 May is the working day: working groups, one big theme every two hours. Friday 28 May is the symposium, on the main stage. Saturday 29 May is the party day. The line-up will be announced. See the three days.

Where does the Hackathon · AI Day take place?

At Escola 42, in parallel with the summit, on 27 and 28 May. The best projects demo on stage. More about the hackathon.

Is nonfungibleconference.com the same event?

Yes. The Non Fungible Conference became NFC Summit, and its site now lives here, on nfcsummit.com.

Tickets

What does a ticket include?

One ticket opens the three days of the summit: FAIR PLAY, Kawaii Summit, the Conference, the Art Factory and the parties, final party included.

How do I buy a ticket?

Tickets are sold on Eventbrite, by card or Apple Pay. The Early Bird ticket is on sale now. Get your ticket on Eventbrite.

Are there tickets for students?

Yes, you will be able to apply for a student ticket. More information soon.

What happens if the event is cancelled?

If the event has to be cancelled for a reason of force majeure, all tickets are refunded.

Taking part

What is the wall?

ON THE WALL is the supporters' wall of the summit: your name, hand-painted on the venue. The names already on it are listed on the home page. onthewall.nfcsummit.com.

I am an artist. How do I show my work?

FAIR PLAY machines are designed by artists: join a shared machine or design your own stand. Apply as an artist.

How do I become a sponsor or a media partner?

A stand, a stage, a night, a table: each world of the summit can carry a partner. See the ways to be there.

Coming to Lisbon

Where can I stay in Lisbon?

The venue is in Beato, on the east side of Lisbon, by the river. Hotel partners will be announced.

I need a letter for my visa.

If you need a visa to enter Portugal, NFC can issue a letter confirming your attendance. Write to support@nonfungibleconference.com.

My question is not here.

Write to support@nonfungibleconference.com.
