FAIR PLAY

Play the art.

A fairground where every stand is an artistic digital activation: a machine with a screen, a score and an artist behind it. It takes the central plaza of Unicorn Factory, and everyone crosses it to get anywhere.

27–29 MayCentral plaza
Apply as an artist

How it works

Play

Pick a machine. Shoot, grab, pose or press start.

Win

Your score goes up on the screen. Each stand has its own leaderboard and prizes.

Keep

You leave with a printed piece that carries the artist's name and universe.

The machines

Sketches. The machines are being designed with the artists.

For artists

A shared machine

Your universe on a machine shared with other artists. Players choose you on screen, and your name is on every print made in your style. 50 places.

Your own stand

A full stand, entirely yours: walls printed with your artwork, your machine inside, your own leaderboard and prizes.

What you take home

Your work in every hand, a new public that meets it by playing, and a share of the plays.
