New in 2027 · FAIR PLAY

You don't look at the art. You play it.

This year the summit becomes a fairground, and it is not an ordinary one. Every machine has a screen, a score and an artist behind it. Art at the service of entertainment, by communities, for communities.

Every stand is an artistic digital activation.
